PIKY interactive QA: you are on a temporary Mac that GitHub destroys when
this session ends.

BEFORE YOU START, ON YOUR OWN MAC
  Quit PIKY (and Raycast or Alfred, if they use Option-Space). A shortcut
  held by an app on your Mac never reaches this one.

ON THIS MAC
  PIKY is not installed. Install it the way a person does:
    1. Double-click "Download PIKY (opens Safari)" in this folder, or type
       the release address into Safari. Allow the download.
    2. Open the disk image in Downloads. Drag PIKY onto Applications.
    3. Open PIKY from Applications. macOS will refuse the first time.
    4. System Settings > Privacy & Security > scroll down > Open Anyway.

  WHEN macOS ASKS FOR AN ADMINISTRATOR'S PASSWORD
    The box shows this Mac's own account name. Replace the NAME with the
    temporary administrator from your session note, and type its password:
        name:      pikyqa
        password:  (in your session note)
    The same name and password work for Accessibility and Screen Recording.

  IN THIS FOLDER
    Files/            fixture files for Finder Picks
    Page/index.html   the QA page for Camera and text Picks (open in Safari)
    TestReceiver      the message box that records what Command-Return delivers
    Results/          put your notes and screenshots here

  Screenshots taken on this Mac (Shift-Command-3 or 4) are saved in Results.

EVERYTHING IN "Results" BECOMES PUBLIC
  It is uploaded as an artifact of a public repository when the session ends.
  Do not put a password in it. Do not photograph the RustDesk window or your
  session note.

DO NOT SIGN IN TO ANYTHING
  No Apple ID, no GitHub, no mail. This Mac is not yours and is not private.

TO END THE SESSION
  Move "END SESSION - delete this file" to the Trash. The evidence is then
  collected and the Mac is destroyed. It also ends by itself when its time
